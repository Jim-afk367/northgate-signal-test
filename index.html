Northgate Signal Systems designs and manufactures vibration and temperature sensors for predictive maintenance programmes in heavy manufacturing. Our hardware and analysis software help plant engineering teams catch bearing wear, misalignment, and thermal drift on rotating equipment before it causes unplanned downtime. We work with discrete manufacturers running continuous production lines who need sensor data they can trust without a data-science team on staff.
